Set the God of Death, Destruction and Disorder. He has been fighting for power and control.

He tried to claim complete power by killing [[Osiris]], which started a feud between him and his nephew [[Horus]].

Set made a deal with [[Myrkul]] to rule alongside him as a god of death in exchange for the soul of Horus.

He uses [[Whiro]] as a conduit to enter [[the Oracle]] and finish his epic with [[Horus]]. After a long battle Set gets interrupted in his conquest by the heroes of [[the Oracle]].

Set ultimately gets killed by The Oracles 5 Heroes and is removed from the world.
