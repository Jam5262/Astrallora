Horus also known as [[The Seer]] to [[The Oracle]] was a god that created the Demi plane.

Horus could see all beings marked with death by his uncle [[Set]], and took them out of the world into a demi plane he created called [[The Oracle]].

Horus resided in the [[Spire of the Gods]] where he simply watched the people he had rescued follow the same path every civilization follows: one of war.

One particular hero stood out as a symbol of victory and a being that everyone in [[The Oracle]] looked up to. [[Kinsai]] the hero was promoted to be Horus's avatar after going to the Spire to attone.

Horus was discovered to be hiding in his own demi plane by a few gods who made their way in through cracks in the world. Eventually [[Myrkul]] and [[Set]] used the being known as [[Whiro]] to send [[The Oracle]] crashing into [[Astrallora]] turning the Demi-plane into a continent of this new world.

Horus then clashed with his uncle [[Set]] where they fought for control of [[The Oracle]]. The Seer fought along side the adventuring party of the land to defeat [[Whiro]] and [[Set]]. Ultimately allowing Horus to return to [[Heliopolis]]
