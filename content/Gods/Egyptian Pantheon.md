	The Egyptian Pantheon persists of gods we would recognise as Egpytian gods. When an Egyptian land is discovered we may refer to them as something different. As of right now, for player consumption this is the Egyptian Pantheon.

The Egyptian pantheon consisted of deities worshiped from an unknown land. Their domain was Heliopolis.

# Deities
- [[Horus]], The god of Protection, Observation and Kingship
- [[Set]], The god of Death, Destruction and Disorder
- [[Osiris]], The god of the dead.
