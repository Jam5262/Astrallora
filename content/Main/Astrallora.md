# About

[[Welcome]] to the world of Astrallora. A young growing world around 350 years old. Although the time frame itself is loose due to early days of civilization not recording information.

The world itself is new and the power balance is all over the place. Wildlife and people are still adapting and fighting for stability. People are still discovering the world and trying to understand more about it.

The setting is loosely based on the [[Forgotten Realms]] but open to a lot of [[Homebrew]]. We will be using mostly pre-existing races, classes and feats. I will try to update sections with relevant information should you need it.
